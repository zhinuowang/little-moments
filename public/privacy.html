Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date] · Last updated: [date]

In short: as a guest, everything stays on your device and we receive none of it. If you sign in with Google, your diary, photos, plans and settings are stored in the cloud so they can follow you to your other devices. We don't show ads, use analytics or tracking cookies, or sell anything. You can ask us to delete your account and all of its cloud data at any time.

1. Who we are

Little Moments is a small, non-commercial journaling app run by [name of the person running Little Moments], based in [country] (“we”, “us”). Contact: [contact email].

2. Two ways to use Little Moments

  • As a guest (no account): your pages, photos, plans and settings are saved only in this browser on this device. We never receive them.
  • With a Google account (optional): your content is also saved to our cloud database so it syncs between your devices.

3. What we collect when you sign in

  • Account details from Google: your email address, Google account ID, and the name and profile-picture link that Google provides, plus when you signed in. The app itself only shows your email.
  • What you create: diary pages (title, text, date, mood, stickers and layout, frame, any place you type, seal), photos you add to pages, plans, and app settings (the name you give Momo to call you, language, sound and motion choices).
  • Pages written before you signed in on a device stay on that device. They are uploaded only if you choose “Upload” for them.

4. Technical information

To deliver the app and keep it secure, our hosting provider (Vercel) and database provider (Supabase) process standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of a request, and keep it in their logs for a limited time. When the welcome screen is shown, your browser checks whether Supabase and Google can be reached.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party trackers. The app uses your browser's storage for: your content (IndexedDB), your language and sign-in session (local storage), and offline copies of the app's own files (a service-worker cache that holds no personal content).

5. How we use it

Only to provide Little Moments: to sign you in, and to save and sync your content between your devices. We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use your content to train AI. We do not look at your diary or photos unless you ask us to (for example, to help with a problem) or the law requires it.

6. Where your data is stored, and who processes it

  • Supabase — sign-in, database and private photo storage, hosted on Amazon Web Services in Oregon, United States (us-west-2).
  • Vercel — hosts and delivers the app's files.
  • Google — confirms your identity when you choose “Continue with Google”.

If you live outside the United States, signing in means your data is transferred to and stored in the United States. Guest mode keeps everything on your device.

7. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Database rules let each account read and change only its own records, and photos are kept in a private storage area that only the owning account can open. Your data is not end-to-end encrypted: our service providers and we, as administrators, could technically access stored data. No system is perfectly secure.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your cloud data is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.
  • When you delete a page or plan in the app, it disappears from your devices, and its contents are removed from the cloud. Only a small deletion marker (its ID, its date and when it was deleted) is kept, so your other devices remove it too.
  • Photo files you remove, or that belonged to deleted pages, stay in private storage until your account is deleted.
  • We currently have no backups, so we cannot restore anything once it is deleted.

9. Copies on your device

Little Moments keeps a copy of your content in each browser you use, so it works offline. Signing out does not remove that copy — it stays in the browser, hidden from the app, so changes not yet saved to the cloud are not lost. On a shared computer, remove it by clearing this site's data in the browser's settings. “Erase everything on this device” in Settings removes only this device's copy; for a signed-in account, the cloud copy remains and downloads again the next time you sign in.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See and copy your data: Settings → “Download a backup” saves what is on that device. You can also ask us for a copy of your cloud data.
  • Correct it: edit anything in the app.
  • Delete it: see section 11.
  • Stop using Google sign-in: you can remove Little Moments' access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK, California, or mainland China), you may have further rights, such as objecting to or restricting processing, and a right to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact us to use any of them.

11. Deleting your account and data

There is no “delete account” button in the app yet. To delete your account:

  1. If you want to keep anything, download a backup first (Settings → “Download a backup”).
  2. Email [contact email] from the Google address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Little Moments account”.
  3. We confirm the request comes from the account owner, then within [30] days we delete:
    • every photo file in your private storage folder;
    • all your diary pages (including ones marked as deleted), photo records, plans and settings;
    • your sign-in account.
  4. We reply to confirm when it is done.

Copies on your own devices are not reachable by us: remove them by clearing this site's data in each browser.

12. Children

Little Moments is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in mainland China). Please don't sign in if you are younger. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes we will also try to let you know in the app.

隐私政策

生效日期：[日期] · 最近更新：[日期]

简单来说：以访客身份使用时，所有内容都只保存在你的设备上，我们收不到任何内容。如果你用 Google 登录，你的日记、照片、计划和设置会保存到云端，方便在你的其他设备上同步。我们不投放广告，不使用统计分析或追踪 Cookie，也不出售任何信息。你随时可以要求我们删除你的账户及其全部云端数据。

1. 我们是谁

Little Moments 是一个小型、非商业的日记应用，由 [运营者姓名]（所在国家/地区：[国家/地区]）运营（下称“我们”）。联系邮箱：[联系邮箱]。

2. 两种使用方式

  • 访客模式（无需账户）：你的日记、照片、计划和设置只保存在这台设备的这个浏览器里，我们不会收到。
  • 使用 Google 账户（可选）：你的内容还会保存到我们的云端数据库，以便在你的设备之间同步。

3. 登录后我们收集的信息

  • 来自 Google 的账户信息：你的电子邮箱地址、Google 账户 ID，以及 Google 提供的姓名和头像链接，还有登录时间。应用本身只显示你的邮箱。
  • 你创作的内容：日记页（标题、正文、日期、心情、贴纸和排版、相框、你填写的地点、印章）、你添加到日记里的照片、计划，以及应用设置（你希望 Momo 怎么称呼你、语言、声音和动画选项）。
  • 登录之前在某台设备上写的日记会留在那台设备上，只有你选择“上传”后才会上传。

4. 技术信息

为了提供应用并保障安全，我们的托管服务商（Vercel）和数据库服务商（Supabase）会处理常规的请求信息，例如 IP 地址、浏览器类型和请求时间，并在其日志中保留一段有限的时间。显示欢迎页时，你的浏览器会检查能否连接到 Supabase 和 Google。

我们不使用统计分析、广告、追踪 Cookie 或第三方追踪工具。应用会使用浏览器存储来保存：你的内容（IndexedDB）、语言和登录状态（本地存储），以及应用自身文件的离线副本（Service Worker 缓存，不含任何个人内容）。

5. 我们如何使用这些信息

仅用于提供 Little Moments：让你登录，并在你的设备之间保存和同步内容。我们不会出售或出租你的信息，不会将其用于广告，也不会用你的内容训练 AI。除非你请求我们查看（例如帮助排查问题）或法律要求，我们不会查看你的日记或照片。

6. 数据存储在哪里，由谁处理

  • Supabase —— 负责登录、数据库和私密照片存储，托管在 Amazon Web Services 的美国俄勒冈州（us-west-2）。
  • Vercel —— 托管并分发应用文件。
  • Google —— 在你选择“使用 Google 继续”时确认你的身份。

如果你身处美国以外的地区（包括中国大陆），登录即表示你的数据将被传输到美国并存储在那里。访客模式下，所有内容都只留在你的设备上。

7. 安全

所有连接都使用 HTTPS。数据库规则确保每个账户只能读取和修改自己的记录，照片保存在只有所属账户才能打开的私密存储空间中。你的数据并非端到端加密：我们的服务商以及作为管理员的我们，在技术上可以访问已存储的数据。没有任何系统是绝对安全的。

8. 保存期限

  • 你的云端数据会一直保存，直到你删除它，或要求我们删除你的账户。
  • 在应用中删除日记页或计划后，它会从你的设备上消失，其内容也会从云端移除。云端只保留一个很小的删除标记（编号、日期和删除时间），以便你的其他设备也将其移除。
  • 你移除的照片，或属于已删除日记页的照片文件，会保留在私密存储中，直到你的账户被删除。
  • 我们目前没有备份，因此一旦删除便无法恢复。

9. 你设备上的副本

Little Moments 会在你使用的每个浏览器中保留一份内容副本，以便离线使用。退出登录不会删除这份副本——它会留在浏览器中、在应用里隐藏，以免尚未保存到云端的修改丢失。在共用电脑上，请在浏览器设置中清除本网站的数据来删除它。设置中的“清除这台设备上的所有内容”只会删除这台设备上的副本；对于已登录的账户，云端副本仍然保留，下次登录时会重新下载。

10. 你的选择和权利

  • 查看和复制数据：设置 →“下载备份”可保存该设备上的内容。你也可以请我们提供你的云端数据副本。
  • 更正数据：在应用中直接编辑。
  • 删除数据：见第 11 条。
  • 停止使用 Google 登录：你可以在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 移除 Little Moments 的访问权限。
  • 根据你所在的地区（例如欧盟或英国、美国加州、中国大陆），你可能还享有其他权利，例如反对或限制处理，以及向数据保护机构投诉的权利。如需行使任何权利，请联系我们。

11. 删除账户和数据

应用中目前还没有“删除账户”按钮。如需删除账户：

  1. 如果想保留任何内容，请先下载备份（设置 →“下载备份”）。
  2. 使用你登录所用的 Google 邮箱发送邮件至 [联系邮箱]，主题为“删除我的 Little Moments 账户”。
  3. 我们确认请求来自账户本人后，会在 [30] 天内删除：
    • 你私密存储文件夹中的所有照片文件；
    • 你的全部日记页（包括已标记删除的）、照片记录、计划和设置；
    • 你的登录账户。
  4. 完成后我们会回复确认。

你自己设备上的副本我们无法访问：请在每个浏览器中清除本网站的数据来删除它们。

12. 儿童

Little Moments 并非面向 13 岁以下（中国大陆为 14 岁以下）的儿童。如果你未满该年龄，请不要登录。如果你认为有儿童登录了账户，请联系我们，我们会删除该账户。
